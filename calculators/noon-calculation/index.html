Formula Reference

Formulas used in this Noon Calculation
Total Revolution Present Counter − Previous Counter
Average RPM Total Revolution ÷ (Running Time × 60)
ConstantREV Propeller Pitch (m) ÷ 1852
ConstantRPM ConstantREV × 60
Propeller Distance ConstantREV × Total Revolution
Propeller Speed ConstantRPM × Average RPM
LOG / OG Speed Distance ÷ Running Time
Slip by Distance (Propeller Distance − Ship Distance) ÷ Propeller Distance × 100
Slip by Speed (Propeller Speed − Ship Speed) ÷ Propeller Speed × 100
Reference: Formulas and terminology follow the supplied Noon Calculation reference material.

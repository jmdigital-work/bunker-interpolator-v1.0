Calculation

Temperature-Corrected Density:

(Density of Fuel Oil @ 15°C) × [1 − {(T − 15) × 0.00064}]

Bunker Mass:

Actual Volume × Temperature-Corrected Density ÷ 1000
Note: The default coefficient of 0.00064 is a simplified shipboard calculation default. It is editable and is not presented as a universally correct coefficient for every fuel type.

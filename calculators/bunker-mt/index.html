Calculation Method

Temperature-corrected density is calculated from density at 15°C using the editable correction coefficient.

ρT = ρ15 × [1 − C × (T − 15)]
MT = V × ρT ÷ 1000

Note: The default coefficient of 0.00064 is provided as a simplified shipboard calculation default. It is editable and is not presented as a universally correct coefficient for every fuel type.
